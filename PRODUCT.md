## Users

- **Retail and distribution partners** (chains, pharmacies, convenience, HoReCa, distributors) evaluating whether to list Fresh Fingers. Job: understand the product and the brand in seconds, judge shelf and margin fit, and start a conversation about listing, samples, or an offer.
- **Investors** evaluating ADA Group / the Fresh Fingers brand. Job: grasp the category insight, the brand concept, the product range, and the company's ambition; then make contact.
- **Not consumers.** The site does not sell and is not a consumer shop. End users (active, social people who want to feel fresh between brushings) appear as the audience the brand speaks to, not as the site's visitor.

Language: Polish only. The English brand lines from the brief (e.g. "FRESH WHEN YOU NEED IT.") are kept verbatim as brand claims inside Polish copy.

## Product Purpose

Fresh Fingers are single-use finger wipes for quickly cleaning and freshening teeth and mouth without water or a toothbrush: a pocket format for all the moments between the morning and evening hygiene rituals. The brand sells the moment, not the wipe: "I'm fresh. I'm ready."

The website exists to present the brand and product to business partners and investors, and to convert their interest into contact. Success = a partner or investor understands "what it is, when it's used, why it's different" within the first screen and reaches out.

## Positioning

**Pocket Oral Freshness.** A lifestyle accessory, not a hygiene tool: the thing that belongs next to your phone, wallet, perfume, earphones and sunglasses. The claim a dental or pharmacy brand could not truthfully make: freshness as readiness for the next social moment (coffee, lunch, meeting, date, party, travel, new people), positioned as fashion × lifestyle × nightlife rather than dentistry.

Brand hierarchy when only three seconds are available: 1. FRESH FINGERS → 2. FRESH WHEN YOU NEED IT. → 3. POCKET ORAL FRESHNESS. Only then the occasions.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Confirmed product facts (from the Fresh Fingers pack artwork):
- Single-use teeth cleaning finger wipes, flavour Mint.
- 10 wipes per box, individually wrapped; sliding ("rozsuwane") box format.
- Sugar-free; requires no water; gently wipe teeth with the pad and discard.
- Material 70% polyester / 30% viscose. Ingredients: peppermint oil 84.7%, menthol 15%, stevioside 0.3%.
- Usage icons on pack: after meal, after coffee, on the go. Shelf life 12 months after opening (PAO symbol).
- Pack disclaimer: "Not a substitute for regular brushing." Keep out of reach of children; do not swallow.
- EAN on the dieline is a placeholder (5901234123457).

Communication constraints (from the brief):
- Communicate positively: freshness, convenience, mobility, comfort, confidence, occasions. Never "we have something to clean".
- Claims about plaque removal, enamel safety, or comparisons with brushing may be used **only after** product tests or documentation confirm them.
- Every material must answer at least one of: WHEN? WHY? HOW DO I FEEL? (best answer: FRESH. CONFIDENT. READY.)

Site constraints:
- No online sales, no cart, no consumer checkout. Primary action is business contact (partnership / investor enquiry).
- Polish only; no i18n routing for now.

Open / undecided:
- Contact mechanism (form vs. email/phone) and who receives partner vs. investor enquiries.
- Whether the sister brands are shown on the site by name or only as a "private label available" capability.
- Whether pricing, MOQ, or trade terms are ever published.

## Product Principles

1. **Three-second clarity.** Every surface leads with FRESH FINGERS → FRESH WHEN YOU NEED IT. → POCKET ORAL FRESHNESS before anything else; partners must "get it" as fast as a shopper at a shelf.
2. **Sell the moment, not the wipe.** Occasions and readiness carry the story; hygiene mechanics support it, never lead.
3. **Accessory, not medicine.** Every choice pushes toward lifestyle and nightlife and away from pharmacy and dentistry.
4. **Only true proof.** Claims, endorsements, and traction appear only when documented; absence is stated or designed around, never filled in.
5. **Built for business conversion.** The visitor is a partner or investor; the path to contact is always visible and frictionless.
