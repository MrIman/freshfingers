# Surface brief — index.html (Fresh Fingers landing page, branch wow-wipe)

Scope: one route, Persuade mode. Audience: Polish retail/distribution partners and investors. Job: understand FRESH FINGERS → FRESH WHEN YOU NEED IT. → one product, four brands; download the deck; contact. Proof: pack facts and brief claims only; packshots are the owner's real product photos (assets/packs-real, cut out with rembg); mascot illustrations are generated renders and labelled as such; no invented traction, tests or testimonials.

Owner's pinned references: marvis.com (white page, black uppercase sans + italic serif accents, giant serif hero caps, hexagon MENU, flourish ornaments, colour-drenched product blocks, marquees, wit) and its layer-wiping intro ("ścieranie warstw"). Build path: code-led (owner: page first, images after). No seed roll: a user-pinned direction. Owner supplied real packshots (12) and four logotypes on 2026-09-07.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The product's own gesture is the site's signature: a finger wipe erases coloured layers. On arrival four moment-words (Kawa. Lunch. Randka. Impreza.) are wiped away one over the other by an animated wipe, the last only partly, and the visitor finishes it with their own pointer or finger before the white page is fully theirs; every brand chapter is wiped in over the previous brand's colour the same way. Refuses fades, slides and generic reveals as transitions.

OWN-WORLD: White paper #ffffff, ink #111111; four brand fields exact to pack colours: Fresh Fingers blue #1f49b5 (mint variant #7fc4b5 on pack), Clean&Go red #e3262b, FreshMate navy #0f2f7a, Finger Fresh lime #c8f542. Bodoni Moda 900 uppercase for the giant hero and the wiped words, Bodoni italic 600 for one accent word per heading, Jost for everything else (caps statements, chips, labels). Ornament: authored filigree symbol on rules and card corners, light curl on button ends. Real photographic packshots with soft drop shadows; the owner's logotypes as chapter marks (white-plated on dark fields, original colours on lime). Mascot cameo in the brandmark, mascot vignettes in the moments row.

STORY: Visitor wipes into the page → giant claim + four real packs on a flourish shelf → statement + six moments → four drenched chapters, each wiped in, logo + real pack pair + chips + suggested shelf → one product (open pack photo, 4 steps, spec) → partners / investors framed cards → contact → footer.

FIRST VIEWPORT (after the wipe, 1440x900): brandmark + hexagon MENU; Bodoni hero "FRESH WHEN / YOU need IT." two lines ~86% width; filigree rule; italic caption; four real packs on the shelf with names; filigree shelf rule; ornament button "Poznaj marki"; marquee top edge inside 900px. Before it: the stage, full-bleed colour layers with one word each, a white wipe sprite, "Pomiń", and after the auto-wipe the hint "Zetrzyj resztę kursorem / Przetrzyj palcem resztę".

FORM: Marvis idiom by owner's pin; signature interaction = destination-out canvas wipe (dense soft dabs, S-path in four passes) used for the arrival stage and for chapter covers; body colour follows the chapter under the viewport middle; packs breathe, main pack tilts with the pointer and parallaxes on scroll; three motion beats only (arrival wipe, chapter wipe + colour, statement rise). Reduced motion and ?static: no stage, no covers, chapters painted directly.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
